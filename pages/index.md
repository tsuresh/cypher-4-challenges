# CYPHER 4.0 — The Recruitment Trials

**Phase 1 · Online, jeopardy-style · Organised by IEEE WIE KDU and GDG Sri Lanka**

---

## The brief

The **Serendib Cyber Task Force** is recruiting.

Across the island, companies moved to the cloud faster than they secured it. A tea
exporter in Nuwara Eliya left a storage bucket open. A ride-hailing app in Colombo
trusts user input a little too much. A telecom operator forgot to lock a folder on
its web server.

We need analysts who can find these doors before real attackers do. This is the
entry trial. Every challenge is one company's cloud slip-up — the flags are your
proof.

## How it works

- **13 challenges**, weighted toward easy and medium. A warm-up, five easy, a
  spread of mediums, one genuinely hard, and a written bonus.
- **Teams of 2 to 4**, from the same university.
- **Every flag looks like** `CYPHER{...}` — submit it exactly as you find it.
- **Every challenge has a free hint.** Hints cost **zero points**. Using one is
  never a penalty — take it the moment you're stuck.

## Scoring

- **Dynamic scoring.** A challenge is worth more while few teams have solved it,
  and its value decays as more do. Early solves are worth more.
- **First blood.** The first team to solve a challenge earns a **+50 bonus**.
- **Ties** are broken by who reached their score first.
- The **scoreboard** updates live.

The **top 10 teams** advance to the onsite finals.

## Getting started

1. **Register** an account, then create a team or join your teammate's.
2. Open **Challenges** once the window opens.
3. Start with **Practice Range** — it teaches you how flag submission works.
4. Read each challenge's hint. They're free, and they name the technique in plain
